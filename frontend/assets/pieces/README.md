# Chess piece sets

## cburnett

The SVGs in `cburnett/` are the `cburnett` piece set used by
[Lichess](https://lichess.org). They are the work of **Colin M.L. Burnett**
and are licensed under
[CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

Source: <https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/cburnett>

Files: `wK.svg`, `wQ.svg`, `wR.svg`, `wB.svg`, `wN.svg`, `wP.svg` and the
matching black pieces `bK.svg`, `bQ.svg`, `bR.svg`, `bB.svg`, `bN.svg`, `bP.svg`.
